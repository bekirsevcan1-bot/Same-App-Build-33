---
name: Primary navigation structure
description: The app uses four primary destinations with a centered create-request action.
---

The primary navigation is intentionally limited to Ana Sayfa, Hesabım, İşlerim, and Bildirimler, with a centered Talep oluştur action. Secondary areas such as settings and map should open from these destinations rather than compete for bottom-nav space.

**Why:** The product needs to feel familiar and easy for a first-time customer; too many equal-weight destinations made account controls and the core customer journey hard to discover.

**How to apply:** Keep web and mobile bottom navigation aligned to these four destinations. Put profile, preferences, logout, and deletion inside Hesabım.
